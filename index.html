01

What does Zoomi do?

Zoomi is a design-led marketing company based in Indiana. We design brand identities and websites, plan marketing strategy, and manage search visibility with our own SEO software, Zoomi Audit.

02

What is Zoomi Audit?

Zoomi Audit is SEO software made by Zoomi. It monitors site health, tracks keywords and rankings, and produces a prioritized list of fixes. It can be added to any Zoomi marketing engagement.

03

What marketing services does Zoomi offer?

Three core services: brand identity (logo, guidelines, messaging), websites (design, build, landing pages, technical SEO), and strategy and growth (positioning, roadmaps, analytics and reporting).

04

Where is Zoomi based?

Zoomi, LLC is based in Indiana, USA.

05

How do I get started with Zoomi?

Email hello@zoomi.co with a short note about your business and what you want to improve.
